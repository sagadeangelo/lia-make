# 05 — Node Sounds

> Great interfaces are not only seen.
>
> They are heard.

---

# Introduction

Sound is part of the interface.

It communicates.

Confirms.

Guides.

Rewards.

A creator should feel every interaction.

Without becoming distracted.

Without becoming overwhelmed.

LIA-Make uses sound with the same care it uses color and animation.

Silence is equally important.

---

# Philosophy

Every sound must answer one question.

"What is the interface trying to communicate?"

If the answer is:

"Nothing."

The sound should not exist.

---

# Audio Principles

Every sound should be:

• Short

• Clean

• High quality

• Soft

• Pleasant

• Memorable

Never annoying.

Never repetitive.

Never noisy.

---

# Sound Categories

Interaction

Connection

Execution

System

Celebration

Warnings

Ambient

AI

---

# Interaction Sounds

## Hover

Purpose

Communicate interactivity.

Sound

Almost inaudible.

A tiny synthetic shimmer.

Duration

20–40 ms

---

## Select

Purpose

Confirm selection.

Sound

Soft click.

Modern.

Digital.

---

## Multi Selection

Purpose

Communicate accumulation.

Sound

Slightly brighter than normal selection.

Never louder.

---

## Drag

Purpose

The node has been lifted.

Sound

Soft "whoosh".

Very light.

Almost like moving a card through air.

---

## Drop

Purpose

The node has been placed.

Sound

Soft impact.

Like placing a precision tool on a workbench.

---

# Connection Sounds

## Connection Start

Purpose

The cable begins.

Sound

Small energy pulse.

Very subtle.

---

## Magnetic Attraction

Purpose

The connector is close enough.

Sound

Tiny magnetic resonance.

The user understands

"You're almost there."

---

## Successful Connection

Purpose

Two compatible systems become one.

Sound

Metallic magnetic click.

Clean.

Precise.

Satisfying.

This becomes one of the signature sounds of LIA-Make.

---

## Connection Locked

Purpose

The workflow is complete.

Sound

Small mechanical confirmation.

Very subtle.

---

## Disconnect

Purpose

The connection has been removed.

Sound

Light metallic release.

Never harsh.

---

# Invalid Connection

Purpose

The connection is impossible.

Sound

Short dry click.

No alarm.

No buzz.

No punishment.

The interface simply says:

"This doesn't fit."

---

# Missing Requirement

Purpose

Something is missing before this connection is valid.

Sound

Neutral.

Small muted tone.

The user understands

"Almost."

---

# Execution Sounds

## Start Workflow

Purpose

Execution begins.

Sound

Energy activation.

Like a machine powering on.

Very soft.

---

## Node Executing

Normally

No sound.

Visual animation is enough.

Optional

Very quiet energy hum.

Only for AI nodes.

---

## Workflow Completed

Purpose

Everything finished correctly.

Sound

Elegant confirmation.

Professional.

Short.

Rewarding.

---

## Workflow Failed

Purpose

Execution stopped.

Sound

Low soft tone.

Not dramatic.

Informative.

---

# AI Sounds

AI has its own sonic identity.

Characteristics

Warm.

Futuristic.

Calm.

Intelligent.

Never robotic clichés.

---

## Thinking

Very subtle evolving texture.

Almost imperceptible.

Suggests reasoning.

Not computation.

---

## Suggestion

A gentle chime.

Invites attention.

Never interrupts.

---

## Finished

Short positive tone.

Elegant.

# The Signature Sound

LIA-Make should have one instantly recognizable sound.

The magnetic connection.

Just as:

Apple has its startup sound.

Nintendo has its menu sounds.

PlayStation has its boot sound.

LIA-Make should be remembered by the sound of two intelligent nodes connecting.

That sound becomes part of the product identity.

The creator should feel:

"I just built something."

Not:

"I clicked a button."

An unforgettable interface is remembered with the ears.

Every silence has purpose.
